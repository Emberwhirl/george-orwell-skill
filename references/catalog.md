# George Orwell — works consulted

Inventory of primary works used to build this skill (research date 20 July 2026; inventory updated 2026-08-20).
Archive filenames in the tables identify the local research copies. **Those full texts are not included in this repository.**

Listed books, essays, columns, and letters/diaries are English-language **[PRIMARY]** texts — Orwell's own words as transmitted by the named archive site. Secondary evidence for circulating misquotations lives in `references/attribution/`.

This catalog covers Orwell's six novels and three standard book-length works of nonfiction, plus essays, columns, and a small number of letters and diary extracts used in the skill. It is not a claim that these were all the books published during his lifetime.

## COPYRIGHT NOTE

Orwell died 21 January 1950. His works entered the **public domain in life+70 jurisdictions (UK, EU, Australia, Canada, Russia, etc.) on 1 January 2021**. However, **US copyright runs 95 years from first publication**, so the later books remain in copyright in the United States — *Animal Farm* (1945) until 2041 and *Nineteen Eighty-Four* (1949) until 2045, with other 1930s-40s works expiring on their own 95-year clocks. The source sites used here (Project Gutenberg Australia, orwell.ru) are hosted in life+70 jurisdictions. Full texts were used for local research only and are not shipped with the skill. Diary and letter texts follow Peter Davison's editions and are reproduced online by the Orwell Prize/Orwell Foundation "Orwell Diaries" project and press reproductions; they are treated the same way.

Notes on provenance:
- Files from "Fifty Orwell Essays" were split from the single Project Gutenberg Australia HTML ebook (0300011h.html, produced by Colin Choat) and converted to plain text; each local research copy carries a source header.
- orwell.ru files were stripped from the site's HTML to plain text; orwell.ru permits educational/non-commercial use of texts with acknowledgement of WWW.ORWELL.RU. Site HTML is not copied.
- gutenberg.net.au book .txt files are unmodified downloads (three are ISO-8859-1 encoded: Homage to Catalonia, The Road to Wigan Pier, Down and Out).
- PGA does not keep eBooks in compliance with a particular paper edition. Electronic readability is not print-edition verification.
- PGA's Fifty Orwell Essays header claims all included essays were first published in Orwell's lifetime; *Such, Such Were the Joys* was first published in 1952. The date in the row below is the one that is true.
- Each local copy was verified non-trivial and spot-checked for a signature phrase (e.g. "Big Brother", "P.O.U.M.", "Moulmein", "If liberty means anything at all").

## Consulted bibliographically, not held as full texts

- **The Orwell Foundation (orwellfoundation.com)** returned "Not Found" to non-browser clients for every essay URL tried; nothing was sourced from it directly (its Orwell Diaries WordPress project was reachable and used).
- Standalone gutenberg.net.au editions of Shooting an Elephant (0200141.txt) and Politics and the English Language (0200151.txt) exist but were not duplicated; the Fifty Orwell Essays versions were used instead.
- *The English People* (1947), the *Partisan Review* London Letters, the 1946 Macdonald letter, the 1949 Henson statement, and the hop-picking / Wigan / Morocco / remaining wartime diaries were consulted bibliographically and are not held as reusable full texts.
